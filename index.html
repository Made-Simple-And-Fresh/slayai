Free · 10 tricks pros actually use

Steal these

The tricks that saved me the most time and money since 2021. Filter by what you need and copy the prompts.

#01 · Save moneyRestyle

Fix the first frame, not the video

Export frame 1, restyle it with your reference image, then let a video-to-video tool carry that look through every frame. Image edits are almost free. Video re-renders are not.

#02 · Save moneyTesting

Test 3–5 seconds at the lowest quality

Only render the full clip once the short test looks right. Free daily credits are for tests, paid credits are for finals.

#03 · Better promptsVeo · Sora

Timestamps turn one clip into a sequence

[00:00-00:03] Wide shot, drone pushes in. [00:03-00:06] Medium shot, she turns. [00:06-00:08] Close-up, she smiles.
#04 · Better promptsAudio

Always add "No subtitles"

Video models with sound sometimes burn captions into the image. Two words at the end of your prompt prevent it. Add captions later in your editor.

#05 · ConsistencyNano Banana

The storyboard grid trick

Ask for a 3×3 grid of film stills in one image. Panels generated together share the same face, light and color. Crop each one and use it as a start frame.

Using the character from the reference image, create a 3x3 grid of cinematic film stills of [your story]. Same character, outfit, lighting and color grade in every panel. No text, no borders.
#06 · ConsistencyCharacters

Write a character bible, never paraphrase it

A fixed 40–60 word description you paste identically into every prompt. Small wording changes create small face changes.

#07 · ConsistencyLong videos

Chain clips from the last frame

Clip limit too short? Start each new clip from the last frame of the one before. Hide the cuts on the beat of the music.

#08 · Go viralDance swaps

Match the framing for dance swaps

If the dancer fills the frame but your character is small, limbs stretch and warp. Crop the character image so the body is the same size and position as the dancer.

#09 · Go viralHooks

Show the transformation in second one

Reveal the restyle in the first second, put curiosity text on screen, and end on a frame that loops back to the start. Watch time is everything.

#10 · Go viralStyle

Turn the model's limits into your style

Faces won't stay consistent? Give your character a helmet, a mask or a balloon head. Some of the most famous AI shorts did exactly that.

    Outlook · our take, not a promise

    What's coming in AI video in 2027

    Where things are heading, based on how fast the tools moved from 2023 to 2026. Each prediction is marked by how confident we are.

    2023Glitchy 2–4 s clips. Faces melt.
    2024Realistic motion, image-to-video goes mainstream.
    2025Native sound & dialogue. Video editing by prompt.
    2026Consistent characters, open models catch up.
    2027From clips to scenes. Our predictions below.
    Likely

    Longer, coherent scenes

    Clips grow from seconds to minute-long scenes with consistent characters and locations, instead of stitching 8-second pieces together.

    Likely

    Edit real footage by just asking

    "Make it night", "change her jacket", "remove the car": prompt-based editing of your own videos becomes a normal feature in everyday editors.

    Likely

    Sound is standard

    Dialogue, lip-sync, music and sound effects generated together with the picture become the default, not a premium extra.

    Likely

    Cheaper per second

    Prices per generated second keep falling and free tiers get more generous. Paying for several subscriptions makes even less sense.

    Possible

    Real-time, interactive video

    Generation fast enough to steer while it plays: live camera moves, interactive stories and game-like worlds you can walk through.

    Possible

    Open models match the big labs

    Free open-source video models, like the Wan family, close the gap for most creator work if you have a decent GPU.

    Likely

    Labels and rules everywhere

    Platforms and laws push clearer AI labels and content credentials. Labelling your work becomes routine, and honest creators benefit.

    Wildcard

    Your own trained style

    Training a model on your own look, characters or brand becomes a one-click feature, so your style travels across every tool you use.

    Predictions are opinions based on trends up to 2026, not insider information.
